BEYOND THE BASELINE

About Tennis Tales

Tennis is a game of points, sets, and matches. But some of its most interesting stories have little to do with the score.

THE STORIES

On the court, and beyond the game.

Tennis Tales is a collection of essays and stories inspired by the people, places, communities, and unexpected moments I encounter through tennis.

Some stories begin with a match. Others begin with a conversation, a teammate, an opponent, a neighborhood court, or something that happens between points. Together, they explore the ways a game can reveal much larger stories about community, competition, friendship, identity, and everyday life.

THE WRITER

Li Qi

I came to tennis relatively late, first as a recreational player and eventually as a devoted student of the game. Along the way, I discovered that tennis offered something else I had not expected: stories.

My writing draws on encounters on and around the court, particularly the communities and characters that make recreational tennis much more interesting than the final score suggests.
